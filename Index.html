What is Work IQ?

Work IQ is the intelligence layer behind Microsoft 365 Copilot and agents. It connects Copilot to your organization's knowledge, context, people and work — so answers reflect your emails, meetings, chats and files, not just the internet.

  • Understands your work — email, meetings and calendar, Teams chats, documents in OneDrive and SharePoint, and the people you work with.
  • Respects what you can see — it works on your organization's data where it already lives and uses only content you have permission to access.
  • Powers every approach on this page — a prompt, an agent and Cowork all draw on the same Work IQ. What changes is how much you set up and how much Copilot does for you.

An agent packages your instructions and trusted content once — so you, and anyone you share it with, get the same approach from a one-line question.

When to use it

  • You ask the same questions about the same content every week or month.
  • You want your team to get answers the same way, without remembering a long prompt or where the files live.
  • One person (a “maker”) builds and maintains it; many people use it.
  • Answers come from knowledge you choose — SharePoint sites, folders and files, uploaded files and websites, and, depending on your license, Teams chats, meetings and email.

When to step up

  • Copilot Studio — when the agent must take actions, run multi-step workflows or approvals, connect to other business systems, or serve a whole department or organization. Usually built with a maker or your IT team. Agent Builder agents can be copied to Copilot Studio.
  • Cowork — when you want a multi-step outcome produced for you (files, drafts, calendar recommendations) rather than answers.

How to build one

  1. 1

    Start a new agent

    In the Copilot app, select New agent.

  2. 2

    Describe it

    On the Describe tab, paste the prompt below — or start from a template.

  3. 3

    Add knowledge

    Under Knowledge, add the SharePoint site, folders or files the agent should use.

  4. 4

    Test it

    On Try it, ask a question you already know the answer to and check the sources.

  5. 5

    Share it

    Share the agent with your team. People still need access to the underlying files to get answers from them.

Leadership review agent

Create-the-agent promptAgent Builder › New agent › Describe
Create an agent called <agent name> for <my leadership team>. It reviews the updates our teams post to <SharePoint site, library or folder>. When asked, it compares the latest files with the previous ones and returns: what changed, themes and watch-outs, conflicting statements, decisions needed with owners and due dates, overdue commitments, and the questions leaders should ask each owner. It cites the file for every point, separates facts from assumptions, never estimates figures that aren't in the files, and says clearly what it couldn't find. Add these suggested prompts: What changed since the last review? What should I ask each owner? What's overdue? Where do the files disagree?

Know the limits

Knowledge sourceLimit per agent
SharePoint files, folders or sites100
SharePoint lists1
OneDrive files50
Files uploaded from your device20
Public websites4 URLs
Teams chats and meetings5 chats and 5 meetings
  • Uploaded files: up to 512 MB for Word, PowerPoint, PDF and text; 30 MB for Excel.
  • SharePoint and OneDrive files you select have no direct size limit, but only supported file types are used — and your organization may set stricter limits. If a large deck causes trouble, saving it as a PDF can help.
  • Excel works best when the data is on one sheet. Keep files concise.
  • New files show “Preparing” and aren't used until they're ready.
  • Permissions and sensitivity labels still apply.
  • “Only use specified sources” prioritizes your content but doesn't fully block general knowledge — Copilot Studio offers stricter control.
  • If your organization uses Restricted SharePoint Search, SharePoint can't be used as knowledge.

Availability: Cowork requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, Cowork enabled in your organization, and usage-based billing turned on. If you don't see Cowork next to Chat in the Copilot app, ask your IT team.

Copilot Chat answers. Cowork does the work: you describe the outcome, Cowork plans the steps, works across Microsoft 365, and checks with you before it acts.

What's new

Outcomes, not answers

Describe what you need. Cowork turns it into a plan, works through it and asks when something isn't clear.

Several deliverables, one request

Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDF files, email drafts and briefings — from a single prompt.

Works across inbox and calendar

It can draft and send email, schedule meetings and manage your calendar — and asks for approval before it sends, posts or schedules.

Runs on a schedule

Ask in plain language — “every Friday at 7 AM”. Cowork confirms and asks for approval before activating it.

Skills you can reuse and share

Save a way of working as a skill, run it with one sentence, and share it with colleagues.

Your permissions, your control

Cowork can access only what you can access. Pause, resume or cancel at any time.

When to use it

Use Copilot Chat when…

  • You need a fast answer, summary or first draft.
  • It's one step in one conversation.
  • You'll take the next action yourself.

Use Cowork when…

  • The work has several steps or several outputs.
  • It spans files, email and calendar.
  • You want it repeated on a schedule — or turned into a skill others can use.

How to start

  1. 1

    Open Cowork

    Open the Copilot app and select Cowork next to Chat.

  2. 2

    Describe the outcome

    Say what you want produced. Select + to add files, people or meetings as context.

  3. 3

    Review the plan

    Answer any questions Cowork asks as it works.

  4. 4

    Approve actions

    Approve or cancel actions such as sending email or scheduling meetings.

  5. 5

    Review the results

    Open the files in the side panel, download them, or keep refining.
